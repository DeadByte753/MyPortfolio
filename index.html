Finora

          
A personal finance app for college students, on the web and on your phone: track your allowance across wallets, set budgets, and save toward goals. Both apps share one database and the same money logic, so an expense added on the phone shows up on the web dashboard right away. Retro pixel-art interface. "Every Peso. Every Purpose."
