About me

From Malinao, building outward.

A student developer who likes knowing how systems work, and how they break.

I'm a 2nd-year BS Information Technology student majoring in Instructional System Technology, studying at Aklan State University – Kalibo.

I got into tech by building things: interfaces in React, mobile apps in Flutter, and a full-stack budgeting app for students like me. Competing pulled me toward security. Capture-the-flag rounds at HackForGov and DICT's ethical hacking mentoring showed me how much is at stake when software is careless with people's data.

My goal is a career in cybersecurity: building secure systems, and helping local governments and schools in the Visayas protect what they run.
